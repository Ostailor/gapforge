## Known Limitations

- GapForge does not guarantee exhaustive search, novelty, publication readiness, benchmark performance, or venue acceptance.
- Live sources can be incomplete, rate-limited, stale, or unavailable.
- PDF and full-text extraction can miss sections, references, equations, tables, and layout.
- Optional LLM outputs remain untrusted until schema-valid, evidence-located, reviewed, and imported.
- Fixture, smoke, and fallback data validate workflow mechanics only; they are not research-quality evidence.
- The accepted v0.9 pilot is an evidence-backed refusal, not proof that GapForge can always produce a defensible research direction.
- Real benchmark, real publication, independent replication, rebuttal closure, and camera-ready workflows remain outside this RC claim.
