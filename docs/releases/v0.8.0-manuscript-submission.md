# GapForge v0.8.0 Manuscript and Submission Status

Release statement: **v0.8 manuscript/submission gate passed for the fixture workflow; no real venue submission happened**.

This file records the manuscript, artifact-evaluation, and rebuttal evidence for v0.8. It distinguishes draft, review-ready, submission-ready, and camera-ready states and avoids claiming publication readiness where gates remain open.

## Readiness Definitions

| State | Meaning | Gate Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | Section text or package text exists. | May contain unresolved review, citation, result, artifact, or traceability work. |
| Review-ready | Humans or simulated reviewers can inspect the manuscript package. | Blockers and uncertainty remain visible; review-ready is not submission-ready. |
| Submission-ready | Venue checklist, citations, traceability, results, artifacts, anonymization, and unsupported-claim checks pass. | Unsupported claims, fake citations, fake results, and missing required artifacts block this state. |
| Camera-ready | Submission-ready plus fatal review/rebuttal blockers are addressed. | Open fatal rebuttal/revision items block camera-ready status. |

## Safety and Honesty Rules

- No fabricated citations are allowed into manuscript citations.
- No fabricated result artifacts are allowed into figures, tables, or result claims.
- Smoke and pilot results must stay labeled and cannot be phrased as main results.
- Strong novelty and SOTA claims require prior-work and benchmark/leaderboard support.
- Known limitations and failed/negative experiments must remain visible.
- An anonymous venue requires anonymization checks before review package readiness.

## Remaining Risks

- The v0.8 fixture does not replace a real manuscript authoring and venue-submission process.
- Venue templates are generic and require human venue-specific review before real submission.
- Reviewer simulation cannot guarantee acceptance or catch all human-review issues.
- Artifact-evaluation package generation does not equal independent artifact acceptance.
- CI remains deterministic/offline and does not require LaTeX installation.

## Not Tested

- Actual submission to any venue.
- Actual arXiv upload.
- Actual reviewer rebuttal with venue constraints.
- External artifact-evaluation committee review.
- Independent third-party reproduction.
- Large manuscript with broad live-literature and benchmark claims.
