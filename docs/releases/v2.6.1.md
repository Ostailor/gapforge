# GapForge v2.6.1 Release Notes

GapForge v2.6.1 is an eval calibration patch. It separates workflow success from paper quality so artifact completeness, matrix loadability, and release-gate mechanics cannot hide a weak manuscript.

## What Changed

- Added grouped eval scores for regression, safety, workflow, paper quality, release gate, and blocker-aware overall status.
- Added paper-quality assessment for the selected benchmark manuscript.
- Added calibration fixtures for misleading pass scenarios: high workflow with low quality, fake citation, copied prose, missing artifact, bad novelty, honest workshop candidate, clean conference candidate, and synthetic deployment overclaim.
- Updated v2.6 release-gate reporting to include paper-quality status, top-conference readiness, workshop readiness, and likely reviewer decision.

## Release Boundary

- v2.6.1 does not add broad new research features.
- v2.6.1 does not change any paper-readiness claim unless the paper-quality assessment supports it.
- v2.6 status remains `workshop_candidate` and not top-conference ready unless a future gate reports otherwise.
- The current v2.6 gate reports `top_conference_readiness=false`, `workshop_readiness=true`, and likely reviewer decision `borderline_reject`.
- Eval now separates workflow success from paper quality; tooling success is progress, not paper success.
